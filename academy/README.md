# The J.A.R.V.I.S. Academy

The Academy is where the white papers are taught as a course. The Academy Bot holds the trilogy — Paper I (the Vision), Paper II (the back end), Paper III (the front end) — and teaches it to HgX in two-hour expert sessions, with every lecture documented, commented, versioned, and published to the repository, the website, and the Dropbox education dossier.

**Cadence:** Tuesday, Wednesday, Thursday — 6:00–8:00 AM America/Toronto.
**Course order:** Paper I → back end (Paper II) → front end (Paper III), the roadmap alongside throughout.
**Standing law:** everything documented, commented, versioned; nothing deleted or overwritten; everything filed in versioned subfolders.
