## What this pack is

How to teach OPERATOR PRIME, at PhD level, for the day-to-day professor in the core spot (Course Bot room). The Dean holds canon, versioning, audits, oversight, and scaling; the professor teaches day to day, marks, gates, and reports. This pack is the professor wiki: every detail, file, and reference the professor needs to teach the whole course, in one indexed place.

## What is in this pack (100 files)

- How to Teach OPERATOR PRIME — Master Guide: 1 file (philosophy, PhD-level standard, daily/weekly/monthly rhythm, assessment, escalation).

- Module Teaching Guides: 15 files (M0–M14, PhD-level depth per module — outcomes, deep concepts, session plan, common errors, evidence standard, marking criteria).

- Weekly Professor Guides: 52 files (W01–W52; W01–W49 follow L1–L49, W50–W52 are the L50 three-week stretch; preparation, day-by-day teaching, homework marking, audit day, pitfalls, gate).

- Monthly Professor Plans: 12 files (progression, checkpoints, Project 25, venues, students at risk).

- Monthly Marking & Feedback Guides: 12 files (criteria only — no exam key, no model answer, ever).

- Quarterly Professor Reviews: 4 files; Final Year Professor Review: 1 file.

- Facility Setup Guides: 3 files (indoor lab, private airfield, private farm).

## Rules that govern every file

- PhD-level standard: defined, reproducible, falsifiable, audited, defended by re-measurement — or it is not passed.

- Blank-results rule: no student answer, measurement, mark, or score is pre-filled anywhere; no exam key, no model answer.

- No year, cohort start date, clock time, holiday date, specific venue name, fee, cover, premium, limit, or pass mark is claimed anywhere — all blank for HgX to lock, or professor decisions on marking with Dean review.

- L21–L50 are drafts, not locked, not taught as locked until HgX locks them expressly.

- Embedded-1.0 correction remains provisional — NOT taught as locked canon until HgX confirms it expressly in the Academy room.

- Insurance, waiver, venue, and data files from Dean batch 2 remain draft templates only, not policies, until Ontario lawyer/broker review and HgX approval in a locked version.

- Nothing in this pack goes to students as taught material until HgX approves the exact content for that use and locks the cohort details it needs. (This pack is published open source on HgX's order — which is why it contains no exam key or model answer at all.)
