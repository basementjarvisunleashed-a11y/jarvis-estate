## The PhD-level standard in this guide

A claim in this course is doctoral-standard only when it meets all five tests, and the professor must hold every one of them, every week:

1. **Defined:** every load-bearing term is defined exactly as taught, with its source stated.
2. **Reproducible:** the method is written so another operator, on the same own devices and own targets, can repeat it and get the same measurement, or a stated reason why not.
3. **Falsifiable:** the claim states what measurement would disprove it.
4. **Audited:** the evidence pack, the gate, and any re-measurement are filed, indexed, and versioned — never overwritten, never deleted.
5. **Defended by re-measurement:** a challenged claim is answered with a new measurement, not with a louder restatement.

If a student's work fails any test, the professor marks that failure plainly, requires re-measurement, and records the gap. That is not harshness; it is the course.
