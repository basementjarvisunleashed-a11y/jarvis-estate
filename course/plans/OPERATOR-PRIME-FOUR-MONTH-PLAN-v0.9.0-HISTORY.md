**Standard: professional, laboratory-only, own devices and targets only, consenting adults only. Every laboratory follows See → Document → Log → Prevent.**

## Daily rhythm (each teaching day)
Each laboratory block is integrated, not theory first and laboratory later as separate courses:
- Day 1 of a block: module teaching and laboratory setup, including the safety and consent checklist for that block.
- Middle days: laboratory execution, with tool, version, command, output, and measurement recorded as the work runs.
- Final day of a block: evidence filing, error analysis, and defence of the result. A laboratory is not complete until its evidence is filed.
- Fridays are evidence and review days within the block, not free days. If a gate fails on Friday, the following catch-up day is used for resubmission, and the next block does not start until the gate is filed or explicitly carried forward with a written reason.

## Phases
- Phase 1, Weeks 1 to 2: Foundations. M0 laboratory rules, consent, and audit; M1 Kali Linux; platform verification; Project 25 Tier 1 (presence and safe self-test for all 21 families).
- Phase 2, Weeks 3 to 14: Laboratories L1 to L16, in order, with their modules taught inside each block.
- Phase 3, Weeks 15 to 16: Laboratories L17 to L19, plus Project 25 Tier 2 verification (lab-target functional tests for the core 15 families, filed during L5, L10, and L16, and verified here).
- Phase 4, Week 17: Capstone. L20 Operator Olympics, evidence pack completion, written report, narrated summary, and capstone defence.

## Contingency and honesty about the fit
The plan fits exactly, with no spare teaching week. The buffer is the three catch-up days, plus the evidence day at the end of each laboratory block. If a laboratory gate fails, it is resubmitted on the next catch-up or evidence day. If a laboratory overruns by more than one day, the overrun is absorbed by compressing report finalisation in Week 17, not by dropping a laboratory, a module, or the capstone defence. No laboratory is skipped to meet the finish date. If the instructor decides a laboratory needs an additional day, the finish date for that laboratory moves, and the capstone defence in Week 17 is protected first.
