## Welcome, operator
You have used computers your whole life. Now you operate them. This course teaches you to sense a system, analyse it, prove what you found, defend it, and report it — with evidence, every time, in that order: See → Document → Log → Prevent.
You will work only in a controlled laboratory and at authorised venues, only on your own devices, tags, networks, and targets, or on targets explicitly authorised in writing, and only with consenting adults, for defensive education. That is not a footnote. It is the course.

## What you will learn (7 learning outcomes, from the syllabus, in student voice)
1. Build and measure a laboratory estate, with ground truth, and quantify your own error.
2. Sense spectrum, cards, Bluetooth, chips, mesh, vision, and aerial systems, with open-source hardware, and prove what you sensed with a filed log.
3. Reverse engineer your own firmware and applications, locally, with REA, and turn a verified finding into a teachable, fully documented presentation.
4. Harden what you broke, re-test it, and file before-and-after results that prove the defence.
5. Build a local knowledge graph of your own findings, and answer a stated question only from filed laboratory findings.
6. Audit an entire laboratory estate in one day, file findings per area, and write a defence brief.
7. Present a capstone defence, verified by re-measurement, with a full evidence pack, report, narration, and defence brief.

## Assessment, in student voice
Evidence pack 40% — every lab, in order, with a filed pack. Written report (GenOffice) 25% — findings, method, error analysis, defence, every claim tracing to a filed capture or log. Narrated summary (VoiceStudio) 15% — narration matching the logged test output exactly. Defence brief and capstone defence 20% — presented, verified by re-measurement. A failed gate is resubmitted until it passes. No mark for fabricated, incomplete, or unverifiable results.

## Rules you sign, in one page
Own targets only. Consenting adults only. Laboratory and authorised venues only. No real-world surveillance or deployment. No non-consenting person tested. No template or recording retained after its exercise — deletion log filed. Radio only on own authorised frequencies. Drone only with a Transport Canada checklist, in a safe controlled area. Lasers Class 1 only. Logs honest, complete, in order, with tool, version, command, output, measurement, error, and file path, for every step. Open source, credited in full: HGX Engineering, HGX Academy, and Jarvis, in that order, code MIT, text CC-BY-4.0, footer on every output. If you cannot show consent, ownership, and a filed log for a test, do not run the test.
