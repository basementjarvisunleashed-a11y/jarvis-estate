## What a portfolio is, in this course
A curated selection from the student's own filed evidence packs, written report, narrated summary, and defence brief — nothing else. A portfolio that contains an unfiled claim, a non-owned target, or a personal datum is not a portfolio, it is a liability, and it is not filed.

## Portfolio structure (template, copy per graduate, scrubbed)
- Cover: course name, HGX Academy, modules completed, labs completed, checkpoints completed, capstone result — all with gate statuses, never with a mark for an unverifiable result.
- Evidence highlights: one finding per area, each with tool, version, command, output, measurement, error, hardening change, and re-test result, each tracing to a filed pack.
- Defence highlight: one defence, verified by re-measurement, with before-and-after results.
- Learning note: what the operator struggled with, what broke, what was fixed, in general terms, with consent, no personal data.
- Limits, stated plainly: what was not tested, what was out of scope and why, what remains draft (for example, draft hardware or draft labs, if any were used).

## What a portfolio never claims
No university affiliation, no external accreditation, no course code, no certification that HGX Academy has not granted in a locked file. No sealed substance, no prompt text, no non-owned target, no non-consenting person, no personal data. If an employer or a defence reviewer asks for a claim the portfolio cannot trace to a filed pack, the honest answer is that the claim is not in the portfolio.

## Filing
A portfolio is filed as its own versioned item, scrubbed, with the student's consent, and reported to HgX through the Dean before any external sharing. External sharing itself requires HgX's explicit approval of the exact portfolio content.
