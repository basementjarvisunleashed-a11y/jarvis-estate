1. **Digital Twin Lab** — iPad LiDAR (5 m, 3D) + RPLIDAR A1/A2/C1 (12 m, 2D) + MLX thermal + OpenMV + Frigate. Scan the same lab room four ways, fuse into a 3D twin with a thermal overlay in CloudCompare/Open3D, then re-scan a week later and detect what changed, what moved, what got hot. Security audit of a room, over time, with proof.
2. **Off-Grid Satellite Alert** — SatNOGS + T-Watch + T-Beam + Acer gateway. A satellite pass is scheduled, the ground station receives it, the Acer pushes the result out over the LoRa mesh — no internet, no cell towers, off-grid alerting. Mesh + space, in one chain.
3. **Rogue Transmitter Hunt** — KrakenSDR (5-channel direction finding) + HackRF (spectrum) + T-Beam mesh nodes. Plant your OWN rogue transmitter in the lab, triangulate it with Kraken, relay its location over the mesh, walk to it, kill it, document the hunt. Find-the-source as a sport.
4. **Dark Lab** — Pi NoIR + MLX thermal + OpenMV + Frigate + RPLIDAR. Lights out, full sensing: near-IR sees, thermal feels heat, LiDAR maps, Frigate confirms person vs shadow — presence WITHOUT identity, fusion map, blind-spot map. What can you still defend when you cannot see?
5. **Card-to-Chip Full Chain** — Flipper (field read) → Proxmark (deep RFID/NFC) → GreatFET (bus) → ChipWhisperer (power + glitch) → REA (firmware analysis) → defense. One own access system, attacked end to end, from the card in your hand to the chip inside, then hardened at every layer. This is the whole course in one chain.
6. **Voice-Controlled Lab** — PipeWire voice layer + wake + speaker gate + Ollama + orchestrator + VoiceStudio. Speak a lab into existence: "Jarvis, scan the room, hunt the rogue, log it" — the lab runs, Jarvis narrates results live, GenOffice writes the report as the lab runs. Lab as conversation.
