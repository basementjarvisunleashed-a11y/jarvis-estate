## Capstone rule, both capstones
Scan an own laboratory estate, break an own target, defend it, report it, present the defence brief. Defence is verified by re-measurement. A defence without re-measurement does not pass. Scoring: evidence quality first, then completeness, then clarity. Speed alone does not win.

## Team roles (capstone day, rotating where applicable)
Operator (runs the test, records command and output), Recorder (owns the evidence log), Auditor (checks gates, verifies re-measurement, files inspection notes). No student audits their own log alone. Team score sheet filed with the pack.

## Capstone day, in order (template, no clock times locked)
Opening (scope, team roles, scoring rule) → scan block (own estate only, findings logged per area) → break block (own target, REA, findings logged, confidence scored before write-up) → defend block (harden, re-test, before-and-after filed) → report block (evidence pack, written report, narrated summary, defence brief, team score sheet) → defence block (presented, verified by re-measurement) → close (full pack filed, deletion logs filed, next capstone day's scope noted).

## L20 Operator Olympics (mid-course capstone)
All twenty hardware pieces, as required, team challenges, scored on evidence quality, not speed alone. Hardware: all H1–H20, as required by each challenge. Venue: farm + airfield for big experiments, indoor for audit and defence, summer heat / storm fallback indoor (template, no date locked).

## L50 Year Capstone (final, year draft only)
Week 1: rehearsal and evidence review, buffer days inside the week. Week 2: team challenges, evidence quality first, buffer days inside the week. Week 3: defence briefs and capstone defence verified by re-measurement, buffer days inside the week. Hardware: all H1–H30, as required, re-measurement only in the final week. Venue: indoor primarily, final audit and defence rehearsal indoors. Close: final evidence pack, final report, final narrated summary, final defence brief filed. No mark for fabricated, incomplete, or unverifiable results.
