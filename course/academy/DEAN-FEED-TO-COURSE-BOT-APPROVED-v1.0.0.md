## What the Dean feeds the Professor
Academy feeds Course Bot, and Course Bot teaches from the feed. Nothing is published to students, or posted anywhere, without HgX's explicit approval of the exact content (standing Course Bot rule, kept).

### Daily feed (per teaching day, in order)
- That day's lab and module, from TEACHING-SCHEDULE-DRAFT-v0.1.0, with its hardware gate(s).
- Opening brief: safety, consent, scope, own targets only.
- Tool check list: Kali, REA, stacks for the day, versions to record.
- Lab steps in order, with evidence to file that day, gate criteria, and resubmission rule for a failed gate.
- Debrief prompt: one finding per student, one inspection note, one correction.
- Close checklist: evidence filed, templates/recordings deleted where required, deletion log filed, next day's hardware gate checked. A day does not close with an unfiled log.

### Weekly feed (per teaching week)
- Week plan in order: 3 lab days, 1 evidence / homework day, 1 audit / inspection / lecture day, buffer days as catch-up only (template, no dates locked).
- Homework pack: reading, REA teardown write-up (one own target), evidence log completion, one hardening answer with re-test plan, Graphify update.
- Audit / inspection pack: weekly lab inspection scope, audit areas for the week, areas out of scope with reason, lecture from live lab evidence only.
- Roles for the week, rotating daily: Operator, Recorder, Auditor. No student audits their own log alone.
- Open threads carried forward: failed gates, resubmissions, buffer days used.

### Monthly feed (per month)
- Month plan in month order, from the teaching schedule draft (January–December order, no year locked, no dates locked).
- Monthly audit scope and findings template, monthly venue inspection template.
- Monthly rollup from YEAR-LOGS-DRAFT-v0.1.0: labs run, gates passed / failed / resubmitted, checkpoints completed, evidence packs filed, open threads.
- Monthly learning summary, scrubbed, with consent, no personal data, no sealed substance: what worked, what broke, what tools were missing, what evidence logs looked like, what operators struggled with, what hardware failed, what workflows were too slow or super fast, and why. Reported to HgX alone, through the Dean, not published.
