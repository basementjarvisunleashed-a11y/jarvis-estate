## What a course like this must think about, before a cohort
- General liability: injury or property damage arising from course activities, in the lab and at a venue. Blank for policy, provider, limit, period, exclusions — to be filed from the actual policy, never invented.
- Equipment: own hardware (H1–H30), loaned hardware, student-owned hardware, damage, loss, theft, and custody — see the Equipment Loan and Custody Pack.
- Venue: private airfield, private farm, indoor lab — venue's own insurance, the course's insurance, and who covers what, in writing, before any venue day — see the Venue Agreement Template.
- Drone: drone liability and Transport Canada compliance, separate from general liability, with its own policy record where required.
- Cyber / professional: what the course does NOT do — no real-world testing, no non-owned targets, no non-consenting persons — and how that scope limit is stated in every student document, so a claim cannot honestly say the course authorised it.
- Student personal cover: students are told, in writing, to check their own cover for personal injury and personal equipment, and that the course does not promise to cover personal equipment loss unless a filed loan record says so.

## Gap checklist, to be closed before a cohort (all blanks until filed)
- [ ] General liability policy filed (provider, limit, period, exclusions) or a written record that none is held and the cohort does not run until it is held.
- [ ] Drone policy filed where required, or drone days removed from the schedule until it is filed.
- [ ] Venue insurance records filed for every venue used.
- [ ] Equipment custody records filed for every loaned piece.
- [ ] Waiver template reviewed by a lawyer, filed as its own version (see the Liability Waiver Template).
- [ ] Incident and emergency plan filed and briefed (see the Incident Report and Emergency Plan).
No cohort, no venue day, and no drone day runs with an open insurance gap in its own checklist. A gap is closed by a filed policy record, not by an assurance.
