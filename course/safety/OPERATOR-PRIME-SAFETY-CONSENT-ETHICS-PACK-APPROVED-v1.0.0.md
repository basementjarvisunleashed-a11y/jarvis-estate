## Consent form template (blank, copy per participant per exercise)
- Exercise and lab code, date-order only (no calendar date locked by this template).
- Participant role: consenting adult participant only. Statement: I consent to this laboratory exercise, on my own devices / targets as listed, for defensive education only, in the laboratory only.
- Scope: what will be recorded, what will be tested, what will be deleted, and when. Templates and recordings are deleted after the exercise. Deletion log filed before close.
- Withdrawal: consent may be withdrawn at any time, and the test stops. Withdrawal is logged, not penalised.
- Never: family, son, strangers, non-consenting persons, real-world surveillance or deployment. If a proposed participant falls in any of those categories, the exercise does not run.

## Safety checklists (copy per day, file before any test)
- Lab: own targets only, isolated own network, safety brief given, hardware gates passed, deletion plan stated.
- Radio: transmit only on own authorised frequencies, spectrum check filed, own transmitter only.
- Drone: Transport Canada checklist completed and filed before flight, safe controlled area, wind / visibility gates passed, no flight in snow or high wind, storm-stop brief given.
- Laser: Class 1 only, verified before use.
- Night / farm / airfield: venue boundaries stated, own targets only, no public or neighbour targets, weather check filed first, fallback indoor plan stated.

## Ethics rule, in one line, for every lab
If you cannot show consent, ownership, and a filed log for a test, do not run the test. If a test would need a sealed mechanism, a non-owned target, or a non-consenting person to be interesting, it is out of scope for this course.

## Incident and stop rule
Stop the test on any consent, safety, or scope failure. Log the stop, the method, and the error honestly. Tell the professor and the Dean through the log. Resume only after the failed check passes and is filed.
