## Risk method (copy per lab, per venue, per cohort)
For each hazard, file: hazard, who is exposed, likelihood before controls, severity before controls, controls in place, likelihood after controls, severity after controls, residual risk, owner, review date-order. A risk with no filed controls is an open risk, and the lab that carries it does not run until its controls are filed.

## Hazard families, course-wide (template rows, fill per lab)
- Electrical / USB / battery: own devices only, cables checked, damaged cells removed from use, charging supervised, no charging unattended overnight.
- Radio / spectrum: own authorised frequencies only, spectrum check filed, no transmission on a frequency the course does not hold authority for, no interference with a neighbour or public service.
- Drone: Transport Canada checklist filed, safe controlled area, wind / visibility gates, no flight in snow or high wind, storm-stop, venue boundaries filed.
- Laser: Class 1 only, verified before use, no Class 2 or above in the course.
- Rover / vehicle: own course only, ground truth markers, obstacle log, speed gate, stop control filed, no public road use.
- Night / farm / airfield: venue boundaries, weather check first, hydration / shade / storm-stop in the safety brief, fallback indoor plan, own targets only.
- Face / voice / biometric: consenting adults only, laboratory only, templates and recordings deleted after the exercise, deletion log filed, never family, never son, never strangers.
- Data: no personal data in logs, no Wi-Fi names, no serials, no passwords, no credentials, no live location, scrubbing before any external sharing.

## Review rule
Risk assessments are reviewed at every monthly venue inspection, after every incident, and before every new lab, venue, or hardware piece. A review files a new version naming what changed. Nothing is deleted or overwritten.
