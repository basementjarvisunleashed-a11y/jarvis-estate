## Purpose
One log system for the whole year, so no day, lab, checkpoint, or month closes with an unfiled log, and so what the year actually taught can be read back honestly at the end of it. Logs are evidence, not stories: every entry carries tool, version, command, output, measurement, error, and file path, where applicable.

## Log levels, in order
1. **Daily log** — one per teaching day. Opening brief, tool check, test blocks, midday review, evidence pack block, debrief, close checklist, deletion log where required, next day's hardware gate check. Signed off only when every log for that day is filed.
2. **Lab log** — one per lab, L1–L50 in draft order. Goal, module(s), hardware gate(s), steps in order, per-step tool / version / command / output / measurement / error, hardening change and re-test result where applicable, gate status: passed, failed (with method and error, resubmission filed), or not yet run. A lab is not complete until its evidence is filed.
3. **Checkpoint log** — one per Project 25 checkpoint, 21 checkpoints (15 core + 6 specialty: GPU, Bluetooth, RFID, SDR, VoIP, Labs). Each entry: tool present, safe self-test on own target, evidence saved, Tier 1 presence + self-test for all 21, Tier 2 lab-target functional tests for core 15. No checkpoint passes on assertion.
4. **Hardware log** — one per hardware piece, H1–H30. Gate per piece: box photo, cable, USB recognised on Kali, safe self-test passed, HgX says go. H1–H20 in hand, opened, tested on HgX's word; H21–H30 draft, openness verified at purchase, closed drops to reference only — a draft piece is never logged as in hand by assertion.
5. **Monthly rollup** — one per month, in month order (January–December, no year locked). Labs run, gates passed / failed / resubmitted, checkpoints completed, audits and inspections filed, buffer days used, venue inspections, evidence packs filed, open threads carried forward.
6. **Quarterly audit log** — one per quarter, 4 per year, draft. Per-area findings, hardening changes, re-test results, areas marked out of scope with reason.
7. **Year rollup** — one per year, at close. Full counts: modules, labs, hardware, checkpoints, audits, inspections, buffers used. Final evidence pack, final report, final narrated summary, final defence brief filed. Open threads listed, never closed by assertion.

## Student learning log — one per lab, and one per month
This is the learning record, kept alongside — never inside — a student's evidence pack, so a student's work is never confused with the course's learning record.
Per lab, scrubbed, with consent, no personal data, no sealed substance:
- What worked, and why, with the measurement that shows it.
- What broke, with method and error, and what the fix or resubmission was.
- What tools were missing, or were the wrong tool for the stated measurement.
- What the evidence logs actually looked like — complete, gappy, or misleading, and where.
- What operators struggled with, in general terms (a step, a tool, a measurement), never as a named student's record.
- What hardware failed, and what the workaround or replacement gate was.
- What workflows were too slow, and what workflows were super fast, and why.
Per month, the lab entries are curated into a monthly learning summary: patterns across labs, repeated tool gaps, repeated measurement errors, and hardening lessons that held up on re-test.
Learning summaries are reported to HgX alone. They are course-improvement input first. If, and only if, HgX confirms the embedded-1.0 correction expressly, they are also the input he uses for later service design — Academy does not build that service alone, and no later-version, sealed, alpha, or prompt material goes into any teaching material or any learning log.

## Scrubbing and consent rules, every log, no exceptions
- No personal data: no real names, no Wi-Fi names, no home addresses, no serials, no passwords, no credentials, no live location, no family details, no financial details.
- Face, voice, and biometric work: consenting adults only, lab only, defensive education, templates and recordings deleted after the exercise, deletion log filed.
- Own devices, own tags, own networks, own targets only. Controlled lab / own venues only.
- No attack prompt text reproduced in any log. Defence commentary only.
- No later-version, sealed, or alpha substance in any log. A log that would need such substance to be honest is filed as "withheld, sealed substance, not logged here" — never filled by invention.

## Filing rules
- A day does not close with an unfiled log. A lab does not close with an unfiled evidence pack. A checkpoint does not close on assertion.
- Logs are filed in versioned order, newest entry appended, nothing deleted or overwritten. Corrections are filed as new entries naming what they correct.
- This draft is its own versioned item. It does not replace, delete, or overwrite any locked file, any prior draft PDF, or any prior year-expansion draft. Year, cohort start date, holiday dates, specific venue names, and clock times are NOT locked by this draft — they are blanks to be filled only when HgX locks them with the cohort.
