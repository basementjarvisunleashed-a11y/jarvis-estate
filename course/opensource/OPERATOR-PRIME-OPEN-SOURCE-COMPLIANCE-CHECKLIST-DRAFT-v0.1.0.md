## Pre-publication checklist (copy per publication, tick only on evidence)
- [ ] Version stated, and the file is filed as a new version, never as an overwrite, never as a deletion.
- [ ] Licence stated: MIT for code, CC-BY-4.0 for text, or both, as filed, with licence headers intact.
- [ ] Full credit stated: HGX Engineering, HGX Academy, and Jarvis, in that order, with the footer @basementjarvisu · basementjarvisunleashed@gmail.com, on the title page, the README, the index, and the website footer.
- [ ] J.A.R.V.I.S. first and prominent, HgX small underneath, real name nowhere in public materials.
- [ ] No university affiliation claimed, no external accreditation claimed, no endorsement claimed.
- [ ] Third-party credit filed: every third-party item in the Third-Party Attribution Register, with licence and credit line as required.
- [ ] Hardware credit filed: every hardware piece credited by name, project, openness status, and licence where filed.
- [ ] Scrubbed: no personal data, no sealed substance, no prompt text, no non-owned target, no non-consenting person, no live location, no family details, no financial details, no credentials.
- [ ] Draft vs locked stated plainly: draft files marked draft, locked files marked locked, planned stacks / labs / hardware marked planned, never presented as built.
- [ ] Academy policy stated: anything built from this course should remain open source, with full credit — stated as Academy policy, not as a legal term of MIT or CC-BY-4.0, unless a copyleft licence has been filed for that work.
- [ ] HgX approval filed: exact content approved by HgX before publication, with the approval record filed beside the publication record.
