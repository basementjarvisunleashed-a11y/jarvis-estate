## What credit is, and what it is not
- Credit is attribution: who made it, who teaches it, and where it came from, with version and licence stated beside it.
- Credit is not endorsement: a reuse that credits HGX Engineering, HGX Academy, and Jarvis does not thereby claim that HGX Engineering, HGX Academy, or Jarvis endorses, accredits, certifies, or partners with the reuse, unless a separate, filed agreement says so.
- Credit is not ownership transfer: crediting a reuse does not transfer ownership of the original, and it does not transfer ownership of a derivative to HGX Academy, unless a separate, filed agreement says so.
- Credit is not optional under the licences: MIT requires the licence and copyright notice to be kept; CC-BY-4.0 requires attribution. Removing credit, shortening it, or burying it is a licence failure, and a reuse that does so is not a compliant reuse.
