## How to cite, in order
Cite the work, the version, the licence, and the credit, every time, in that order. A citation without a version is an incomplete citation. A citation without credit is a licence failure.

- Single file: "[File title], version [version], in OPERATOR PRIME, by HGX Engineering, HGX Academy, and Jarvis (J.A.R.V.I.S.), licence [MIT / CC-BY-4.0], @basementjarvisu · basementjarvisunleashed@gmail.com."
- Lab or module: "[Lab / module code and title], version [version], in OPERATOR PRIME, by HGX Engineering, HGX Academy, and Jarvis (J.A.R.V.I.S.), evidence pack filed under [pack reference, where applicable]."
- Derivative: "[Derivative title], version [version], derived from OPERATOR PRIME version [version], by HGX Engineering, HGX Academy, and Jarvis (J.A.R.V.I.S.), changes stated in [changelog reference], licence [licence]."

## What never to cite
Never cite a draft as locked, never cite a planned stack, lab, or hardware piece as built, never cite a sealed item, never cite a personal datum, and never cite a university affiliation or an external accreditation that has not been filed.
