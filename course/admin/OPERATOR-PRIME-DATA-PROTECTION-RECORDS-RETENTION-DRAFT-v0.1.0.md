## Minimisation, everywhere
Collect only what a lab, a gate, a safety check, or an audit strictly needs. No real names in published copies (roles only), no Wi-Fi names, no serials, no passwords, no credentials, no live location, no family details, no financial details, in any log, report, portfolio, or website copy. Scrub every output before any external sharing, and file the scrub check.

## What is kept, and for how long (template, final schedule to be locked on legal review)
- Evidence packs, logs, and gate records: retained for the cohort's full record, versioned, never deleted or overwritten, with corrections filed as new versions.
- Consent forms, waivers (once approved), incident reports, and inspection notes: retained under the final locked retention schedule, with access limited to the Dean, the professor, and HgX.
- Templates and recordings from face / voice / biometric exercises: deleted after the exercise, deletion log filed, never retained beyond the exercise.
- Student learning summaries: scrubbed, consented, no personal data, reported to HgX alone, retained as course improvement records only.

## Deletion and breach
Deletion, where required, is logged: what was deleted, when (date-order), by whom (role), and under what rule. A deletion that is not logged is a failed log. A suspected data breach stops external sharing, is logged the same day, is told to the Dean and to HgX, and is reviewed before sharing resumes.
