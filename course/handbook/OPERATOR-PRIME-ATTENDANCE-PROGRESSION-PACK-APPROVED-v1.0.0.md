## Attendance (template, per day, per student, roles in published copies)
- Present / absent / catch-up, with reason filed where a day is missed, and a catch-up plan filed on a buffer day. No new teardown on a buffer homework day — catch-up only.
- A missed lab day is not a passed lab day. A missed gate is resubmitted until it passes, on a buffer day or a homework day, with its own evidence pack.

## Progression (in order, no skipping)
Labs are completed in order, one at a time. A student progresses to the next lab only when the current lab's gate has passed and its evidence pack is filed. A student progresses to the next module only when every lab in the current module has passed. A student progresses to a capstone only when every lab before it has passed.
Holiday / religious observance / illness catch-up: buffer days exist for exactly this purpose, and a catch-up plan is filed, not assumed. No student is penalised for an observance or illness that is filed honestly and caught up on a buffer day.

## Withdrawal and pause
A student may pause or withdraw, in writing, with a filed decision, and with their evidence packs retained under the retention pack. A paused student returns only through a Dean-approved catch-up plan, starting from the last passed gate, never from an unpassed gate by assertion.
