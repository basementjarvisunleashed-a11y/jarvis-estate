## Complaint (any student, any day)
File in writing, with lab code, date-order, what happened, and what remedy is sought. The professor logs it the same day, the Dean reviews it, and a filed decision follows, naming what was found, what was decided, and what changes, if any, follow. A complaint about a safety, consent, or scope matter stops the affected lab until the Dean's review is filed.

## Grade / gate appeal
A student may appeal a failed gate, a mark, or a conduct decision, in writing, with the evidence pack and the decision being appealed attached. The Dean reviews the pack, not the assertion, and decides: gate stands, gate resubmitted with a named correction, or gate re-marked with a filed reason. A re-mark files a new version naming what changed. No appeal decision is filed by assertion alone.

## Final appeal
If a student disagrees with the Dean's appeal decision, the final appeal goes to HgX, founder, HGX Academy, in writing, with the full pack. HgX's decision is final, filed as its own version, and reported to the student. No other appeal route is promised by this template, and no external accreditation body is named, because none is claimed.
