## Conduct, in the lab, at a venue, and in writing
- Own targets only, consenting adults only, laboratory and authorised venues only, spine SEE → DOCUMENT → LOG → PREVENT, no exceptions.
- No testing of a non-owned target, no recording of a non-consenting person, no real-world surveillance or deployment, no reproduced attack prompt in any log, report, or portfolio.
- Honest logs only: tool, version, command, output, measurement, error, file path, for every step. A fabricated, incomplete, or unverifiable result is a conduct failure, not a technical error, and is filed as failed with a resubmission plan.
- Respect for people, equipment, and venues: own and loaned hardware returned in the state recorded at loan, venues left as found, other students' logs and evidence not altered, never deleted, never overwritten.
- No personal data in any course material: no real names, no Wi-Fi names, no serials, no passwords, no credentials, no live location, no family details, no financial details.

## Breach and consequence (template, Dean-approved)
- Minor breach (late log, incomplete checklist): correction filed, resubmission, inspection note.
- Serious breach (non-owned target, non-consenting recording, fabricated evidence, safety scope failure): lab stopped, incident report filed, Dean review, lab gate failed until the correction and re-test pass, and, where the breach is repeated or deliberate, removal from the laboratory day or the cohort, on the Dean's filed decision, with a right of appeal under the Complaints and Appeals Pack.
- Every breach decision is filed as a new version, naming what happened, what was decided, and what appeal route applies. No decision is filed by assertion alone.
