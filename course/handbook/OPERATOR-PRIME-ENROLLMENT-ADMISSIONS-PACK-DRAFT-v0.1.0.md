## Eligibility (template, Dean-approved per cohort)
- Consenting adult, with a background in non-destructive testing within materials engineering, or an equivalent background approved by the Dean in a filed decision.
- Able to work independently in a laboratory, to file honest evidence logs, and to follow safety, consent, and scope rules.
- Own devices and targets, or access to the course's loaned hardware under the Equipment Loan and Custody Pack, for the duration of the cohort.
- No prior Kali or REA experience assumed, but independent laboratory work required.

## Application pack (copy per applicant, blanks only)
- Eligibility statement, background statement, laboratory experience statement, consent to the course rules, safety brief acknowledgement, and data minimisation acknowledgement — no personal data beyond what the application strictly needs, and no personal data in any published copy.
- Admission decision: admitted / waitlisted / declined, with reason filed, Dean-approved, and a right of appeal under the Complaints and Appeals Pack where a decision is declined.
- No admission promise, no accreditation promise, no employment promise, no fee promise is made by this template. Fees, if any, are filed as their own locked record per cohort, never invented here.

## Cohort record
One cohort record per cohort: admitted students (roles only in any published copy), labs in order, hardware gates, venue inspections, audits, and the final rollup, all versioned, all retained under the Data Protection and Records Retention Pack.
