## Accessibility (template, per student, filed privately, never published)
- Accommodation request form (blank): what is needed, for which labs, for how long, with a Dean-approved plan filed, and a catch-up plan on buffer days where a lab cannot be completed in its standard form.
- Lab access: indoor lab, farm, and airfield days each state their access, terrain, weather, and fallback indoor plan in advance, so a student can plan honestly. A lab that cannot be completed accessibly in its standard form is completed through a Dean-approved equivalent, with its own gate and evidence pack, never by waiving the gate.
- Materials: lecture notes in written and narrated form, visuals with text descriptions, logs in plain language, no claim without a log entry, in either form.

## Inclusion and observance
- Religious holiday, illness, and family emergency catch-up: buffer days exist for exactly this purpose, a catch-up plan is filed, and no student is penalised for an observance or illness that is filed honestly and caught up on a buffer day.
- Language: course materials in English, in plain language, with the glossary for every technical term. A term that is not in the glossary is defined in the lab that introduces it, and the glossary is corrected as a new version.
- Respect: no personal data, no family details, no financial details, in any course material, and no student is asked for a datum the course does not strictly need.
