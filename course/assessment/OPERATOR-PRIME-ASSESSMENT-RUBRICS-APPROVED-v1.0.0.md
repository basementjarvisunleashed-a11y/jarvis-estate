## Evidence pack — 40%
- Complete: every lab in order, checklist filed, tool log complete, measurements quantified, error against ground truth stated where applicable.
- Partial: a lab missing a capture, a log, or a re-test, with the gap named and a resubmission filed — partial credit only for the filed part, never for the missing part by assertion.
- Fail: fabricated, incomplete, or unverifiable evidence; a gate marked passed without its log; a pack with a deleted or overwritten original. Resubmit until it passes.

## Written report (GenOffice) — 25%
- Complete: findings, method, error analysis, and defence, with tracked revisions, every claim tracing to a filed capture or log.
- Partial: a report that states a finding without its measurement, or a defence without its re-test.
- Fail: a report that reproduces an attack prompt, names a non-owned target, or carries a personal datum.

## Narrated summary (VoiceStudio) — 15%
- Complete: clear, professional narration of method and result, matching the logged test output exactly.
- Partial: narration that adds a claim not in the log, or omits a material error.
- Fail: narration that presents a failed gate as passed.

## Defence brief and capstone defence — 20%
- Complete: capstone presented, defence verified by re-measurement, before-and-after results filed, team score sheet filed. Evidence quality first, then completeness, then clarity. Speed alone does not win.
- Partial: a defence presented without re-measurement, or a re-measurement without its error analysis.
- Fail: a defence that cannot be re-measured, or a capstone pack with an unfiled log.

## Gate and resubmission rule, all components
A failed gate is resubmitted until it passes. A resubmission files the new evidence as a new version, naming what it corrects. No mark is awarded for a fabricated, incomplete, or unverifiable result, at any weight.
