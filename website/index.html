What this is

A personal AI, built in the open as a matter of foundation — not as a release strategy. One principal. A federation of narrow roles. Current facts, inspectable evidence, and human approval at every consequential boundary.

The system goes public; the principal's private life never does. That boundary is absolute.

The doctoral programme (eight phases) commissions the proof: frozen contracts, a verified state machine, invariant verification, and a defensible evidence package — each phase gated, versioned, and inspectable on GitHub.
